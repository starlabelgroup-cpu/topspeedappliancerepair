Straightforward pricing

Know the structure before we arrive

No invented totals and no work without your approval. Here is exactly how residential pricing works:

  • $99 diagnostic (residential). Collected at booking and credited toward your approved repair. If you decline the repair, the diagnostic is still due — it covers the technician's visit and diagnosis.
  • Repair labor: $210 flat. One flat labor rate for the repair, agreed in writing before work starts.
  • Installation: $150 flat. Flat labor for appliance installation; parts and materials are separate.
  • Parts & materials at written quote. Priced in your written quote — and no work is ever done without an approved written quote.

Commercial and property-management service is priced by custom quote — visit Appliance Pro, our commercial portal, or call (954) 931-7997. Payment is due on completion unless agreed in writing. Travel is included across our South Florida service area; any extra travel is disclosed and approved in advance.
